# My Smaller Projects and Contributions

In this section you can find some other things I did and some projects I contributed to.

## PeriodicCli

View the periodic table in your terminal. I originally made this because of my chemistry class and the fact braille periodic tables are incredibly annoying and inefficient to use.

[Learn more and download](/projects/periodic-cli)

## Minecraft Access

A blind accessibility mod for Minecraft

I made quite a few contributions to it. I did things like added the object tracker, added bow aim assist, wrote the landing page of the website and more.

[Check out Minecraft Access](https://mcaccess.org)

## Audiopub

A free and open source audio sharing platform. Think YouTube, but instead of video it's audio.

I did things like adding bios and the custom audio player. I also created a helper app for streaming to the platform called Audiopub Stream Helper that allows you to have things like sound alerts when listeners join and leave and custom chat commands.

[Check out Audiopub](https://audiopub.site)

[Check out Audiopub Stream Helper](https://github.com/Molitvan/audiopub-stream-helper)
